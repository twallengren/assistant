Toren’s Assistant connects Toren Wallengren’s Google Workspace account to a privately operated Hermes personal assistant.

What it does

The integration supports email review and drafting, calendar planning, finding files, and working with documents and spreadsheets. Access depends on the permissions granted in Google’s authorization screen.

The assistant can also perform account actions when instructed or authorized by its owner. Relevant information may be processed by configured third-party AI providers to answer requests and support these tasks.

For personal use only

This is not a public service. There is no public registration or Google sign-in on this website. These pages explain the integration; they do not provide access to the assistant or publish any connected account data.
